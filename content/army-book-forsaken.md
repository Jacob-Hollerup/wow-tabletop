## FACTION IDENTITY

**Theme:** Chemical warfare, poison, and plague. Free-willed undead who weaponize alchemy and biological agents against the living. The Royal Apothecary Society turns the battlefield itself into a weapon.

**Playstyle:** Debuff and attrition through two signature conditions — **Poison** (damage over time) and **Plague** (stat degradation). Apply both to a target and the combination becomes devastating. Blight tokens deny ground. Dark Rangers snipe from Stealth. The longer the battle goes, the weaker the enemy becomes. Most models are Undead (immune to Morale, cannot be healed). Apothecaries are Living — functioning minds needed to brew toxins.

**Faction Mechanic — Blight:** Forsaken models with the **Plague** keyword can place a Blight token within 3" when they activate (free, once per activation). Enemy models that move through or end their activation on Blight suffer 1 automatic wound (DEF save allowed). Blight persists for 2 rounds (3 rounds if placed by the Grand Apothecary).

## FORSAKEN STATUS EFFECTS

The Forsaken inflict two signature conditions. These are central to the faction's identity and interact with each other.

### Poison

_Applied by: Deathstalker melee, Poisoned Blades equipment, Black Arrow, Toxic Injection_

When a Poison source deals at least 1 unsaved wound (unless the ability specifies otherwise), the target becomes **Poisoned**:

- The Poisoned model suffers **1 wound at the start of its next activation** (DEF save allowed).
- Poison lasts 1 round but can be reapplied.
- If the target is also **Plagued**, the Poison wound **ignores DEF saves**.

### Plague

_Applied by: Plague Vial (Apothecary), Plague Bomb, Blight Spray, Dread Charge, Plague Burst_

When a Plague effect hits a target, it becomes **Plagued**:

- A Plagued model suffers **-1 TGH** until end of round.
- Plague can be reapplied each round but the TGH penalty does not stack beyond -1.

### The Synergy

If a model is both **Poisoned AND Plagued**:

- The Poison wound at start of activation **ignores DEF saves** — the plague has weakened the body beyond resistance.
- The -1 TGH from Plague makes the target easier to wound with normal attacks too.
- Abilities that reference "Poisoned or Plagued" targets gain enhanced effects (see Adaptive Toxin).

This is the Forsaken's core strategic loop: apply Plague to weaken, apply Poison to kill, and let the combination guarantee damage every round.

## FORSAKEN DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

**Scourge raises the dead. Forsaken breaks the living.**

Two conditions define the Forsaken gameplan:

**POISON** — damage over time. Applied by Deathstalkers, Poisoned Blades, Black Arrow, Toxic Injection, and Apothecary buffs. Deals 1 wound at the start of the victim's activation. The drip damage that finishes off weakened targets.

**PLAGUE** — stat degradation. Applied by Apothecary Plague Vials, Dread Rider charges, Plague Bombs, Abomination death bursts, and Grand Apothecary abilities. Reduces TGH by -1, making enemies easier to wound with normal attacks.

**THE SYNERGY:** When a target is both **Poisoned AND Plagued**, the Poison wound ignores DEF saves. This means:

- **Round 1–2:** Apothecaries soften targets with Plague Vials (-1 TGH) and deploy Blight. Deathstalkers infiltrate under Stealth. Deathguard advance in formation.
- **Round 3–4:** Plagued targets are easier to wound. Poisoned-AND-Plagued targets take guaranteed damage every round with no save. Adaptive Toxin stacks -1 ATK on top.
- **Round 5–6:** The cumulative damage is catastrophic. A TGH 5 Tauren, now Plagued to TGH 4, is taking guaranteed Poison wounds with no save every round while its offense crumbles from debuffs.

**The Undead/Living split:** Undead models (Deathguard, Dread Rider, Dark Ranger, Abomination, Deathstalker, Dark Lady) never break but can never be healed. Living models (Apothecary, Grand Apothecary) can be healed but test Morale. Protect your Living support — without Apothecaries, the Plague pipeline dries up.

**Unique mechanics no other faction has:**

- **Poison + Plague Synergy** — the only faction with two interlocking status effects
- **Blight** — persistent area denial tokens
- **Plague Vial debuff** — stat degradation from a ranged attack
- **Poison** — persistent damage-over-time from melee
- **Plague Burst** — Abominations spread Plague on death
- **Meat Hook** — forced enemy repositioning
- **Charm / Possession** — turning enemies against themselves
