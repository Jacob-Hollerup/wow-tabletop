# AZEROTH AT WAR — Faction Overview

*5 grand factions, 8+ subfactions (and growing)*

---

## GRAND FACTIONS

| Grand Faction | Base | Subfactions | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Alliance** | Humans (Stormwind) | Humans, Dwarves, Night Elves | Active |
| **Horde** | Orcs (Orgrimmar) | Orcs, Tauren, Forsaken, Darkspear Trolls | Active |
| **Scourge** | Scourge (Lich King) | Scourge | Active |
| **Burning Legion** | TBD | TBD | Planned |
| **Void / Old Gods** | TBD | TBD | Planned |

### How It Works

1. **Choose a Grand Faction** — this determines your base battleline and faction mechanic
2. **Choose a Subfaction** — this adds subfaction-specific heroes, elites, additional battleline, and a subfaction mechanic
3. The **base faction** (Humans for Alliance, Orcs for Horde, Scourge for Scourge) is also its own subfaction — picking it gives you the full base roster

## ALLIANCE

**Base:** Humans of Stormwind
**Faction Mechanic — Devotion Auras:** Buff abilities cast by Human models cost 1 less Mana (minimum 1).

### 1. HUMANS (Stormwind) — Base Subfaction
**Theme:** Disciplined, versatile, faith-powered. The backbone of the Alliance.
**Playstyle:** Balanced and adaptable. Strong defensive line, paladins for Taunt/healing, solid ranged crossbowmen, good cavalry for flanking. Wins through discipline and flexibility.

**Units:**
| Unit | Tier | Role | Key Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paladin | Hero | Tank/DPS | Protection or Retribution specs. Versatility racial (2 abilities/activation). Lay on Hands. |
| Archmage | Hero | Caster/Damage | Frost, Arcane, or Fire specs. Versatility racial. Blizzard/Pyroblast/Arcane Power. |
| Footman | Baseline | Core Infantry | Shield Wall formation. Sword+Shield or Halberd loadouts. |
| Crossbowman | Baseline | Core Ranged | 24" range, STR 4. |
| Knight | Mounted | Heavy Cavalry | MOV 8, Momentum [1]. Sword+Shield or Halberd loadouts. |
| Priest | Elite | Support/Healer | Heal, Inner Fire, Smite, Resurrection (once per game). |
| Sorceress | Elite | Caster/Control | Frostbolt (slow), Polymorph (disable), Invisibility (stealth). |
| Champion | Elite | Melee Leader | For Lordaeron! (+1 ATK to chargers), Inspiring Presence. |

**Strengths:** High DEF across the board, efficient buffs, versatile roster.
**Weaknesses:** Low INI (heavy armor everywhere), no Regeneration or Berserker, predictable.

---

### 2. DWARVES (Ironforge / Wildhammer)
**Theme:** Stubborn, heavily armed, ranged firepower and aerial prowess.
**Playstyle:** Ranged-dominant with the best guns in the game. Extremely tough and stubborn (highest Morale). Slow-moving but devastating at range.

**Subfaction Mechanic — Stoneborn Resolve:** Dwarven models add +1 to Morale tests. Dwarves almost never break.

**Units:**
| Unit | Tier | Role | Key Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mountain King | Hero | Melee Bruiser | Storm Bolt, Avatar/Last Stand. Stoneform racial. Battle Cry once per game. |
| Dwarf Ranger | Baseline | Core Ranged | 30" rifle range, RSTR 5. Best base range in game. |
| Ironforge Guard | Baseline | Core Infantry | MOV 4, TGH 5, DEF 4+. Shield wall. |
| Thane | Elite | Heavy Infantry | Decisive Blow [2]. Devastating melee. |
| Gryphon Rider | Elite (Mounted+Elite) | Flying Shock Cavalry | Fly, Momentum [1]. Dual-slot. |

**Strengths:** Best ranged game, highest Morale, tough infantry, gryphon air support.
**Weaknesses:** Slowest faction (MOV 4 most infantry), limited magic, few Magical units.

---

### 3. NIGHT ELVES (Kaldorei)
**Theme:** Ancient, stealthy, nature-bound. Guerrilla warfare and druidic shapeshifting.
**Playstyle:** Hit-and-run. High INI across the board, Stealth on many units, strong archery. Druids shapeshift (bear = tank, cat = assassin).

**Subfaction Mechanic — Shadowmeld:** At round start, Night Elf models that didn't attack/use abilities last round gain Stealth until they act.

**Units:**
| Unit | Tier | Role | Key Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| Priestess of the Moon | Hero | Ranged/Support | Sentinel or Elune specs. Searing Arrows, Starfall. Scout once per game. |
| Keeper of the Grove | Hero | Caster/Summoner | Balance or Restoration specs. Force of Nature (treant summon), Tranquility. Innervate once per game. |
| Sentinel | Baseline | Skirmisher | Stealth, MOV 6, INI 5. 18" throwing glaives + melee. |
| Archer | Baseline | Core Ranged | 24" longbow, SKL 3+, INI 5. Stealth. |
| Druid of the Claw | Elite | Shapeshifter | Bear Form (Taunt, Regeneration) or Cat Form (Disengage, Stealth, Decisive Blow [2]). |

**Strengths:** Highest INI faction, Stealth everywhere, excellent archers, shapeshifting versatility.
**Weaknesses:** Low DEF (light armor), fragile if caught in the open, weak against Armor Piercing.

## HORDE

**Base:** Orcs of Orgrimmar
**Faction Mechanic — Blood Fury:** Once per game during Mana Phase, all Orc models gain +1 ATK die and +1 MOV this round.

### 4. ORCS (Orgrimmar) — Base Subfaction
**Theme:** Savage, honorable, shamanic. Brute force meets elemental magic.
**Playstyle:** Aggressive and straightforward. High STR and ATK across the board. Berserker on many units. Shaman heroes provide elemental magic.

**Units:**
| Unit | Tier | Role | Key Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| Far Seer | Hero | Caster/Shaman | Elemental, Enhancement, or Restoration specs. Blood Rage racial. Reincarnation once per game. |
| Warchief | Hero | Melee Leader | Warchief (buff army) or Blademaster (personal killer) specs. Blood Rage racial. Blood Fury once per game. |
| Orc Warrior | Baseline | Core Infantry | MOV 6, Berserker, Momentum [1]. Axe+Shield / Dual Axes / Greataxe loadouts. |
| Wolf Rider | Mounted | Fast Cavalry | MOV 10, Berserker, Momentum [1]. Axe+Shield or Throwing Spear. |
| Orc Shaman | Elite | Caster/Support | Magical. Lightning Bolt, Earth Shield. |
| Kor'kron Elite | Elite | Heavy Infantry | SKL 3+, TGH 5, Cleave. Masterwork weapons (AP 2). |
| Wind Rider | Elite (Mounted+Elite) | Flying Shock | Fly, Momentum [1]. Envenomed Spear (applies -1 ATK debuff). Dual-slot. |

**Strengths:** Highest raw ATK/STR, Berserker everywhere, strong charge game, good elemental magic.
**Weaknesses:** Medium armor at best, below-average DEF, short-range shooting, vulnerable to kiting.

---

### 5. TAUREN (Thunder Bluff)
**Theme:** Mighty, spiritual, nature-guardians. Walking walls of muscle.
**Playstyle:** Elite-heavy, low model count. Every Tauren model is big, tough, and hits hard. Highest base TGH and WND in the game.

**Subfaction Mechanic — War Stomp:** Once per round, one Tauren model may declare War Stomp during activation. All enemies within 2" suffer -1 to hit on next activation. Free, no Mana.

**Units:**
| Unit | Tier | Role | Key Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| Elder Shaman | Hero | Caster/Tank | Elemental or Restoration specs. War Stomp racial. Reincarnation once per game. |
| Brave | Baseline | Core Infantry | MOV 5, TGH 5, WND 4, Cleave. Massive for a Baseline unit. |
| Tauren Warrior | Elite | Heavy Infantry | TGH 6, WND 4, DEF 4+, Cleave. Highest TGH in game. |
| Sunwalker | Elite | Tank/Support | Taunt, Magical, healing, survivability focus. |

**Strengths:** Toughest models in the game, Cleave everywhere, War Stomp disruption.
**Weaknesses:** Very low model count (expensive), slow, no flying, no Mounted, limited ranged.

---

### 6. FORSAKEN (Undercity)
**Theme:** Cunning, vengeful, plague and shadow. The dead who chose to fight back.
**Playstyle:** Attrition and debuff-focused. Plague mechanics weaken enemies over time. Dark Rangers for elite ranged. Split Undead/Living roster.

**Subfaction Mechanic — Blight:** Models with Plague keyword place Blight tokens within 3" when activating (free, once/activation). Enemies moving through suffer 1 wound (DEF save allowed). Persists 2 rounds (3 if Grand Apothecary).

**Status Effects:** Poison (wound on target's next activation, auto if also Plagued). Plague (-1 TGH, reapplicable, caps at -1).

**Units:**
| Unit | Tier | Role | Key Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dark Lady | Hero | Ranged/Shadow | Dark Ranger or Banshee specs. SKL 2+, Stealth, Decisive Blow [2]. Undying Will racial. Charm once per game. |
| Grand Apothecary | Hero | Plague Caster | Plague Doctor or Blightcaller specs. Blight lasts 3 rounds. Plague Mastery racial. New Plague once per game. |
| Deathguard | Baseline | Core Infantry | Undead. Sword+Shield / Dual Blades / Greatsword. Optional Poisoned Blades upgrade. |
| Dread Rider | Mounted | Pursuit Cavalry | Undead, Fear, MOV 8. Dread Charge (apply Plague, force Morale test). |
| Dark Ranger | Elite | Stealth Sniper | Undead, SKL 2+ (most accurate ranged), Stealth, Decisive Blow [1], 24" longbow. |
| Deathstalker | Elite | Melee Assassin | Undead, Stealth, Disengage, Poisoned Daggers. |
| Abomination | Elite | Heavy Tank | Undead, TGH 5, WND 5, Cleave, Fear. Meat Hook. Plague Burst on death. |
| Apothecary | Elite | Plague/Poison Support | Living, Magical, Plague keyword. Plague Vial, Toxin Application. |

**Strengths:** Debuffs that cripple enemy stats, Blight area denial, Stealth assassins, Undead ignore Morale.
**Weaknesses:** Low raw damage, Undead can't be healed, split Morale, moderate base stats.

---

### 7. DARKSPEAR TROLLS
**Theme:** Voodoo, regeneration, berserker fury, primal savagery.
**Playstyle:** Regeneration and Berserker everywhere. Trolls get tougher to kill as the fight goes on. The longer the battle lasts, the more they dominate.

**Subfaction Mechanic — Voodoo Shuffle:** Darkspear models that pass a Morale test gain +1 ATK die until next activation.

**Units:**
| Unit | Tier | Role | Key Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shadow Hunter | Hero | Caster/Support | Voodoo or Shadow specs. Hex, Healing Wave. Regeneration [1] racial. Big Bad Voodoo once per game. |
| Troll Berserker | Baseline | Aggressive Infantry | MOV 6, Berserker, Regeneration [1]. |
| Headhunter | Baseline | Ranged Skirmisher | MOV 6, Berserker. 12" Throwing Axe (RSTR 4). |
| Raptor Rider | Mounted | Fast Cavalry | MOV 10+, Berserker, Regeneration [1]. |
| Witch Doctor | Elite | Caster/Support | Magical. Ward summons, voodoo debuffs. |

**Strengths:** Regeneration everywhere, Berserker + Voodoo Shuffle escalation, strong voodoo magic, good mobility.
**Weaknesses:** Light armor across the board, vulnerable to burst damage, no heavy armor or shields.

## SCOURGE

**Base:** Scourge (Lich King)
**Faction Mechanic — Raise Dead:** When any non-summoned model is destroyed within 6" of a Scourge Magical model, spend 1 Mana to place a Skeleton Warrior at that position.

### 8. SCOURGE (Lich King) — Base Subfaction
**Theme:** Undeath, overwhelming numbers, necromantic power, winter's chill.
**Playstyle:** Swarm and elite. Cheap expendable Undead flood the board. Behind them, terrifying elites bring devastating power. Necromancers constantly raise more troops.

**Hero Bodyguard System:** Lich (3 Acolytes), Death Knight Mounted (2 Ghouls), Death Knight On Foot (2 Skeletons), Necromancer Elite (2 Acolytes).

**Units:**
| Unit | Tier | Role | Key Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| Death Knight | Hero | Mounted or On Foot | Unholy, Frost, or Blood specs. Death Coil. Dark Ritual once per game. |
| Lich | Hero | Master Caster | Frost or Necromancy specs. Frost Nova. Phylactery (4+ return at 1 WND). Dark Ritual. |
| Skeleton Warrior | Baseline | Swarm Melee | Undead, cheapest unit in game. WND 1. Sword+Shield. |
| Crypt Fiend | Baseline | Ranged/Melee Hybrid | Undead, Spider Climb. Venom Spit 18". Web (once per game, ground flyers). |
| Ghoul | Baseline | Fast Aggressive Melee | Undead, MOV 7, Berserker. Glass cannon. |
| Acolyte | Baseline | Support/Mana Battery | Living, Magical. Provides Magical keyword for Raise Dead and Dispels. |
| Necromancer | Elite | Caster | Magical. Plague or Shadow specs. Can take 2 Acolyte bodyguards. |
| Death Knight | Elite | Melee Leader | Undead, Magical. Frost, Unholy, or Blood specs. |
| Abomination | Elite | Monster/Tank | Undead, TGH 6, WND 7, Cleave, Fear. Meat Hook. Plague Burst on death. |

**Strengths:** Entire army Morale immune, endless summoning, overwhelming activations, Fear on elites, frost debuffs.
**Weaknesses:** Undead can't be healed, individual models weak (except elites), no shields, dependent on Magical units for Mana engine.
