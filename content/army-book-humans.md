## Version 0.2 (Full Unit Profiles & Points)

*For the Alliance!*

---

## FACTION IDENTITY

**Theme:** Disciplined, versatile, faith-powered. The backbone of the Alliance.

**Playstyle:** Balanced and adaptable. Strong defensive line with heavy armor infantry, supported by paladins (Taunt/healing) and priests (buffs). Solid ranged with crossbowmen. Good cavalry for flanking. No glaring weakness but no extreme specialty either — they win through discipline and flexibility.

**Faction Mechanic — Devotion Auras:** Buff abilities cast by Human models cost **1 less Mana** (minimum 1). Represents the inspiring faith and discipline of Stormwind.

### Paladin *(Human Hero)*

**Tier:** Hero | **Archetype:** Tank / DPS
**Keywords:** Magical
**Specs:** Protection or Retribution

| MOV | SKL | TGH | DEF | INI | WND |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 3+ | 5 | 4+ | 3 | 5 |

| Weapon | ATK | STR | AP | DMG |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Runesword (SW) | 4 | 5 | 1 | 3 |

**Armor:** Plate Armor (HA)
**Other Equipment:** Shield (Protection spec only, +1 DEF → 3+)

The quintessential Alliance Hero — faith, plate, and a blessed blade. Protection Paladins are unkillable walls that absorb punishment and protect allies. Retribution Paladins are wrecking balls that channel the Light into devastating melee strikes. Human Versatility lets the Paladin double-cast, combining heals with combat abilities in the same activation.

**Racial — Versatility:** May use 2 abilities per activation (second costs +1 Mana).
**Once Per Game — Lay on Hands:** Fully heal one friendly model within 2".

| Level | Global | Protection Spec | Retribution Spec |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lv.1 | Holy Light | Shield of the Righteous | Judgment |
| Lv.2 | — | Inspiring Presence, Taunt | Seal Stance, Crusader Strike |
| Lv.3 | — | **Divine Shield** | **Avenging Wrath** |

**Ability Definitions:**
- **Holy Light** *(2 Mana — Instant):* Restore 2 WND to a friendly model within 6".
- **Shield of the Righteous** *(1 Mana — Reaction):* Negate 1 incoming wound to this model.
- **Inspiring Presence** *(Passive):* Friendly models within 6" gain +1 Morale.
- **Taunt** *(Passive):* Gain the Taunt keyword permanently.
- **Divine Shield** *(Once Per Battle):* Immune to all damage until end of round.
- **Judgment** *(2 Mana — Spell Shoot):* Range 12", STR 5. Target suffers -1 DEF until end of round.
- **Seal Stance** *(Free Action):* Choose one seal at the start of this model's activation. Lasts until next activation:
  - **Seal of Command** — +1 ATK.
  - **Seal of Protection** — +1 DEF.
  - **Seal of Light** — Heal 1 WND at start of activation.
- **Crusader Strike** *(1 Mana — Instant):* Next melee attack this activation gains +2 ATK dice.
- **Avenging Wrath** *(Once Per Battle):* Gain +2 ATK dice and +1 STR until end of round. All abilities cost 0 Mana this round.

Master of the arcane arts. Glass cannon — devastating spell damage but fragile. Human Versatility lets the Archmage double-cast, chaining Fireball into Frostbolt or unleashing Arcane Power combos. Three schools of magic offer distinct playstyles: Frost controls the battlefield, Arcane fuels sustained output, Fire delivers explosive burst damage.

**Racial — Versatility:** May use 2 abilities per activation (second costs +1 Mana).
**Once Per Game — Evocation:** Immediately gain 4 Mana.

| Level | Global | Frost Spec | Arcane Spec | Fire Spec |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lv.1 | Fireball | Frostbolt | Arcane Missiles | Ignite |
| Lv.2 | Arcane Intellect | Ice Barrier | Brilliance Aura | Combustion |
| Lv.3 | — | **Blizzard** | **Arcane Power** | **Pyroblast** |

**Ability Definitions:**
- **Fireball** *(2 Mana — Spell Shoot):* Ranged attack, STR 5, Range 18".
- **Arcane Intellect** *(2 Mana — Buff):* Target Magical model generates +1 Mana next turn. *(1 Mana with Devotion Auras.)*
- **Frostbolt** *(2 Mana — Spell Shoot):* Ranged attack, STR 4, Range 18". Target suffers -2 MOV until end of round.
- **Ice Barrier** *(2 Mana — Buff, self):* Absorb the next 2 wounds dealt to this model.
- **Blizzard** *(Once Per Battle — 3 Mana):* Large Blast (5"), STR 4, Range 18". All hit targets cannot move next activation.
- **Arcane Missiles** *(2 Mana — Spell Shoot):* 3 separate attacks against one target, each STR 4, Range 18".
- **Brilliance Aura** *(3 Mana — Buff):* Army generates +1 base Mana this round. *(2 Mana with Devotion Auras.)*
- **Arcane Power** *(Once Per Battle):* Next 3 Spell Shoot abilities this round deal double damage.
- **Ignite** *(Passive):* When this Hero's Fireball kills a model, the fire spreads — one enemy model within 2" of the killed model suffers a STR 4 hit.
- **Combustion** *(2 Mana — Buff, self):* All Spell Shoot abilities this round gain +2 STR. *(1 Mana with Devotion Auras.)*
- **Pyroblast** *(Once Per Battle — 3 Mana):* Ranged attack, STR 8, Range 18", AP 2.

### Footman

**Tier:** Baseline | **Archetype:** Core Infantry
**Keywords:** Shield Wall

| MOV | SKL | TGH | DEF | INI | WND |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 4+ | 5 | 4+ | 3 | 2 |

**Armor:** Plate Armor (HA)

The Alliance backbone. Plate armor and disciplined formation make them the toughest baseline unit in the game. Individually solid — TGH 5, DEF 3+. In Shield Wall formation, interlocked shields grant a second chance against glancing blows. Their offense comes from the leaders who inspire them — Champions, Paladins, and Priests turn this defensive line into an unstoppable advance.

**Shield Wall:** When 2 or more Footmen are within 2" of each other, Footmen with Shields may **reroll one failed DEF save of 1** per attack. Halberd Footmen count toward the formation requirement but do not receive the bonus.

The Alliance's battlefield healer and inspirational support. Heal keeps models alive, Inner Fire turns allies into offensive threats, and Smite provides surprising ranged damage. Resurrection is the ultimate comeback ability.

**Heal** *(1 Mana — Instant):* Restore 1 WND to a friendly model within 6". A model can only be healed once per round by this ability.

**Smite** *(2 Mana — Spell Shoot):* Ranged attack, STR 4, Range 18".
**Resurrection** *(Once Per Game — 3 Mana):* Return one destroyed friendly non-Hero model to play with full WND, placed within 6" of this Priest.
